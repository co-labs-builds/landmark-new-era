Show up rested and ready.

A few simple agreements that make the Forum work for everyone.
  • Be present for the full day, start to finish.
  • Arrive on time for each session and stay through the close.
  • Give the room your full attention — set phones, devices, and other distractions aside.
  • Keep what others share confidential and treat everyone with respect.
  • Please don't record, screen-capture, or share any part of the event.

Above all — come ready to engage.

There's nothing to study or prepare in advance — just come ready to engage fully. Between now and Friday, keep considering what you'd like to be different, and what you'd love to create. Bring your real life with you: the relationships, the situations, and the parts of your life that matter most.

Good to
know.
What do I need to join?
A computer or tablet with a reliable internet connection, a working camera and microphone, and a quiet space. We'll send a direct link, and you'll enter the Forum right from this page.
When should I log in each day?
The room opens at 8:30 AM Pacific. Plan to arrive at least 15 minutes before the 9:00 AM start so your technology is set and you're settled when we begin.
What if something comes up during the weekend?
The Forum is designed to be experienced in full, so we ask that you arrange your schedule to be present for all sessions. If you have a concern, reach out to our team below and we'll help.
Do I need to take notes or prepare anything?
There's nothing to study or prepare in advance. You're welcome to keep a notebook nearby, but you don't need to capture everything — you'll receive materials, and the value is in being present.
What should I have ready in my space?
Water, nourishing food and snacks, anything you need to be comfortable, and a way to minimize interruptions. Treat it as time set aside just for you.
Who do I contact if I need help?
Our support team is here throughout. You'll find our email and phone in the Contact section just below.

Support

Taking care of yourself

This weekend can be full and rich, and sometimes big feelings show up along the way — that's a natural part of looking honestly at your life. Whatever comes up for you is welcome here.

Simple ways to take care

A few things make a real difference: make sure you're getting good sleep, eating and drinking well, and regularly standing up to move your body. Take a deep breath and look around the room when you need to. Take care of yourself along the way, and reach out whenever you need to.

You don't have to do it alone

If something feels like a lot, please reach out — to a friend, to someone you trust, or to a member of our team. We're here and always glad to support you. Just contact us anytime.

If you'd like to talk to someone right now

Landmark is a personal and professional development company, not therapy or medical care. If you're in distress or simply want immediate, confidential support, these free resources are available any time, day or night:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    Call or text 988 · 24/7 · free & confidential
  • Crisis Text Line
    Text HOME to 741741 · 24/7 · free
  • SAMHSA National Helpline
    Call 1-800-662-4357 · 24/7 · treatment referral & information
  • Find a Helpline
    findahelpline.com · free, confidential support in your country
In any life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.
