What is the Advanced Course?
A 3-day weekend plus a Tuesday evening, open to Landmark Forum graduates. Where The Landmark Forum was about discovering what's been running your life, the Advanced Course is about authorship — designing the future you want to live into.
When should I take the Advanced Course?
Whenever you're ready. Many graduates find the momentum from their Forum weekend is a powerful thing to build on and register within a few months — but the course is offered year-round, online.
Where do I find my certificate and materials?
On the All Programs tab above — The Landmark Forum now shows as completed, with your certificate available to download any time.
How is the Advanced Course delivered?
Online — a 3-day weekend plus a Tuesday evening, so you can take it from wherever you are. It's the same complete, immersive experience as your Forum weekend.
How do I register for the Advanced Course?
Click View Advanced Course Dates above to see upcoming weekends and reserve your spot. If you'd like help choosing a date, our team is glad to talk it through with you. See our contact information below.
What if I need to transfer my course?
No problem — plans change. Contact us at info@landmarkworldwide.com or +1 (800) 888-9590, and for a small $35 administrative fee we'll transfer you to another course date that works for you.
Who do I contact if I have questions?
Our support team is here for you. You'll find our email and phone in the Contact section just below.

Support

Taking care of yourself

This weekend can be full and rich, and sometimes big feelings show up along the way — that's a natural part of looking honestly at your life. Whatever comes up for you is welcome here.

Simple ways to take care

A few things make a real difference: make sure you're getting good sleep, eating and drinking well, and regularly standing up to move your body. Take a deep breath and look around the room when you need to. Take care of yourself along the way, and reach out whenever you need to.

You don't have to do it alone

If something feels like a lot, please reach out — to a friend, to someone you trust, or to a member of our team. We're here and always glad to support you. Just contact us anytime.

If you'd like to talk to someone right now

Landmark is a personal and professional development company, not therapy or medical care. If you're in distress or simply want immediate, confidential support, these free resources are available any time, day or night:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    Call or text 988 · 24/7 · free & confidential
  • Crisis Text Line
    Text HOME to 741741 · 24/7 · free
  • SAMHSA National Helpline
    Call 1-800-662-4357 · 24/7 · treatment referral & information
  • Find a Helpline
    findahelpline.com · free, confidential support in your country
In any life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.
