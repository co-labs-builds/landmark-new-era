Taking care of yourself

This weekend can be full and rich, and sometimes big feelings show up along the way — that's a natural part of looking honestly at your life. Whatever comes up for you is welcome here.

Simple ways to take care

A few things make a real difference: make sure you're getting good sleep, eating and drinking well, and regularly standing up to move your body. Take a deep breath and look around the room when you need to. Take care of yourself along the way, and reach out whenever you need to.

You don't have to do it alone

If something feels like a lot, please reach out — to a friend, to someone you trust, or to a member of our team. We're here and always glad to support you. Just contact us anytime.

If you'd like to talk to someone right now

Landmark is a personal and professional development company, not therapy or medical care. If you're in distress or simply want immediate, confidential support, these free resources are available any time, day or night:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    Call or text 988 · 24/7 · free & confidential
  • Crisis Text Line
    Text HOME to 741741 · 24/7 · free
  • SAMHSA National Helpline
    Call 1-800-662-4357 · 24/7 · treatment referral & information
  • Find a Helpline
    findahelpline.com · free, confidential support in your country
In any life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

Thank you for bringing yourself to this experience. Take good care.
